Rank LadderPromotions

Advancement follows the national Bangladesh Scouts rank structure. Each rung below is a tested, examined step — not a birthday gift.

  1. 01

    Apprentice Scout

    Entry rank on joining the troop. Oath taken, uniform issued, patrol assigned.

    Standard intake
  2. 02

    Second Class Scout

    Tested on knotting, map & compass, first aid basics and troop history.

    Badge examined
  3. 03

    First Class Scout

    Pioneering, endurance hike, and a supervised service project in the community.

    In session
  4. 04

    Rover Scout

    Senior rank for scouts aged 17+; district leadership training and mentoring duties begin here.

    Requires First Class
  5. 05

    Presidents Rover Scout Award

    The group's highest honour — awarded to fewer than one scout per year, on record since 1972.

    Committee nomination
